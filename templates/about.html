About RoomMate Finder

Finding a good roommate is hard. Students search across Facebook groups, Kijiji posts and group chats with no way to compare people, and mismatched habits often lead to conflict.

RoomMate Finder matches University of Windsor students on compatibility, not just rent: budget, area, sleep schedule, cleanliness and more. Verified students earn a badge after an admin checks their student ID, and contact details are shared only after a request is accepted.
